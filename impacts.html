Fossil-heavy electricity generation increases CO₂ and co-pollutants like PM2.5 and NOx. Load shifting to cleaner hours can reduce local air pollution and improve long-term health.

Key Health Outcomes

  • Lower risk of asthma exacerbations from reduced particulate pollution.
  • Reduced cardiovascular stress linked to cleaner air quality.
  • Improved respiratory outcomes for vulnerable communities.

Why Timing Matters

Running deferrable loads (EV charging, dishwashers, laundry) during high-renewable hours cuts emissions and often lowers utility bills.
